Logo library

Partner, platform, client and community marks, as they appear across rbaconsulting.com. Every one in color, one-color black and one-color white, as an SVG or a 512px PNG.

info

These are other people’s trademarks. Use them to say who we work with — a partner wall, a client list, a platform row — and follow the owner’s brand guidelines on clear space and minimum size. Don’t recolor them, and don’t imply a partnership we don’t have. RBA’s own mark lives under RBA logos, not here.

Using one of these

RuleRequirement
Which formatSVG unless something refuses it. The PNG is 512px on its long edge, on transparency — fine for a slide, too small for print.
Which colorwayColor on white or a light surface. Black where the layout is one-ink, or where a row of partner logos in six different brand colors would fight the page. White on a dark fill or over photography — it is the same silhouette as the black one, so a row stays consistent whichever way the section flips.
Don’t recolorBrand colors belong to their owners. Color, black or white — those are the three, and the two one-color cuts are already the sanctioned way to drop the color.
Clear spaceEvery viewBox is trimmed tight to the artwork, so the file gives you none. Add at least the height of the mark’s shortest letterform on all sides.
Sizing a rowMatch logos on optical weight, not bounding box. A wide wordmark and a square badge set to the same width will not look the same size — scale until they read evenly.
AccessibilityGive the image real alt text naming the company. Mark it alt="" only when the name is already in adjacent text.
Provenanceassets/logo-library/MANIFEST.csv records where every file came from and whether it was traced. Check it before using a mark somewhere it matters.

Where these came from

Harvested from rbaconsulting.com, then identity-checked by eye — the alt text on the source site is copy-pasted between logos and cannot be trusted. 30 were already vectors; 88 were traced from raster with potrace, one color at a time. A trace is an approximation: for any brand still in active use, the owner’s own SVG beats anything derived from a web PNG. See assets/logo-library/README.md for the full account, including the ten files that were deliberately not traced.
